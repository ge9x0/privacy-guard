Privacy Guard · for iPhone and iPad

What your apps really do

Your iPhone keeps a record of which apps used the camera, microphone, location, contacts and photos, and which servers they contacted. This page reads that record and points out what is worth a look.

The file is read only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded; the page has no analytics and works offline once loaded.

1. Save the report on your iPhone

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → App Privacy Report and turn it on if it is off. It needs a few days to collect data.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Save App Privacy Report, then Save to Files.

2. Open it here

The file is called something like App_Privacy_Report_v4_2026-11-21.ndjson. On a computer you can also drop it here.

Summary

Worth a look

Use at night, long recordings, frequent location and contacts access, and contact with data brokers. An entry means "check this", not proof of spying: many apps have good reasons.
